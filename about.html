About Du-port Road Baptist Church School

Our school is dedicated to providing a safe and inspiring environment for students to grow academically, morally, and spiritually.

Our History

Since its founding, Du-port Road Baptist Church School has served the community with a commitment to academic excellence and Christian values. We are proud to have graduated generations of students who contribute positively to society.

Mission, Vision & Core Values

Mission

To provide a nurturing and faith-based learning environment that empowers students to achieve academic excellence and character development.

Vision

To be recognized as a leading school in Liberia where learners care for one another and succeed academically.

Core Values

  • Integrity
  • Respect
  • Community
  • Excellence

Accreditation & Affiliations

Du-port Road Baptist Church School is accredited by the Ministry of Education and partners with national and international educational organizations to ensure high standards.
